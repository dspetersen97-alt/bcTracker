Split out of includes/form.html so a page that has to lay a control out beside a particular field — the "set recurring" link next to the date on the scheduling page — can place fields itself without reimplementing how errors and help text are shown. A div rather than a p: widgets that render a list of choices emit their own divs, which a browser would close the paragraph around. And those widgets get a fieldset and a legend instead of a label, because a label pointing at a group of checkboxes points at nothing a screen reader can focus. ``use_fieldset`` is Django's own answer to which is which. {% endcomment %}
