The three shapes

  • Closed all day — leave both times blank and leave the box unticked.
  • Closed for part of a day — give the times, leave the box unticked.
  • Extra time — give the times and tick the box.

These combine. Closing a whole day and then opening 4pm to 5pm means the office is shut except for that one appointment.

Appointments already booked are not cancelled by a closure. You will still see them in your diary, and cancelling one is a separate decision.
